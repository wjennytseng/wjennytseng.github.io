01About

From the training room
to the whole organization.

For five years I’ve led learning and leadership development at Edwards Vacuum, partnering with functional leaders across Taiwan and Southeast Asia to turn development needs into programs that work.

Before that, I supported 30 franchise schools as an academic supervisor, recruited teachers internationally, and led a small instructional team. Each role taught me the same lesson: how much a person grows depends on the context around them — their manager, their team, and the way their organization makes decisions.

That is the question I want to pursue next. I’m preparing for graduate study in organizational behavior and leadership, with the goal of moving into organizational analysis and leadership consulting.
